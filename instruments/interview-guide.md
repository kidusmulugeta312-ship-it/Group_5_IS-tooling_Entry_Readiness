# Follow-up interview guide — draft
Target: 4–6 consenting tech leads and experienced freelancers, as proposed. This is a planned number, not interviews completed.
1. What tool-related tasks are expected from a new graduate in your setting?
2. Which tasks most often require additional support? Ask for a non-identifying example.
3. What training, mentorship, or self-learning resources are available?
4. How do tool gaps influence delivery, collaboration, supervision, or client communication?
5. Which curriculum activities could most directly address these difficulties?
6. What differences do you observe between firm and freelance pathways? Separate firsthand experience from speculation.
Record only with separate permission. De-identify notes; log coding decisions and consider contradictory examples. Do not claim thematic saturation from a predetermined small sample without justification.
