# Proposal revision checklist — not yet completed
- Define the local geographic market and participating institutions.
- Confirm participant eligibility, sampling strategy, target sample size, and recruitment dates.
- Separate final-year students (perceived preparedness only) from graduates with actual workplace outcomes.
- Define firm, freelance, hybrid, and not-yet-employed pathways; do not force hybrid participants into one group.
- Specify a primary outcome measured identically across the two comparison groups.
- Distinguish tool exposure, current proficiency, and required proficiency; exposure and importance are not interchangeable measurements.
- Validate the literature and complete reference metadata before claiming a localized research gap.
- Pre-specify covariates, missing-data rules, and the final statistical analysis before inspecting outcomes.
- Confirm consent, storage, access, and dissemination procedures with the instructor.
