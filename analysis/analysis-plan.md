 #Analysis plan — Draft, Review before data collection
1. Validate eligibility, consent, duplicates, ratings, category membership, and missingness. Report recruitment and exclusions.
2. Summarize exposure and importance separately. These have different meanings and should not simply be subtracted to claim a measured curriculum gap.
3. Rank current skill mismatch by mean(required proficiency minus current proficiency) on complete rating pairs within categories. This is a current self-reported mismatch, NOT direct evidence of curriculum causation. Report sample sizes. Review whether an entry-time measure is needed to align with the research questions.
4. Optional RII = sum(importance ratings)/(5 * count of observed importance ratings). It ranks perceived importance, not the gap itself.
5. Summarize the provisional four-item readiness mean once per respondent, requiring all four answers. Assess scale validity and reliability separately before confirmatory use.
6. The supplied script reports readiness descriptively by pathway. Formal firm/freelance comparison requires prespecified eligibility, suitable sample size, scale checks, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and appropriate statistical tests. Hybrid and not-working participants are not silently reclassified.
7. Differential association requires more than a difference in group means. Consider a prespecified tool-gap × pathway interaction model with justified covariates (experience, internships, independent learning) and diagnostics. Aggregate category scores at the participant level before participant-level regression; do not treat repeated tool rows as independent people.
8. Analyze interviews separately with a coding log, de-identified excerpts, and contrasting cases, then integrate findings with survey patterns.
9. No causal conclusions from the proposed cross-sectional design. Document sampling bias, self-report, recall, confounding, and small-sample limitations.
No hypothesis tests or regression are implemented in the starter script. No results exist yet.
